Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth Whitening: A Brighter Smile, Safely Done

A brighter smile is one of the most requested — and most misunderstood — treatments in dentistry. Done properly, professional whitening is safe, effective and kind to your teeth. Done carelessly with the wrong products, it can leave you with sensitivity and patchy, unnatural results.

Why teeth darken

Teeth pick up colour over the years from tea, coffee, red wine, tobacco and simply from age, as the enamel thins and the darker layer beneath shows through. Some staining sits on the surface; some is deeper within the tooth.

In-clinic and take-home options

In-clinic treatment such as Zoom lifts the shade noticeably in a single supervised appointment, while custom take-home trays let you whiten gradually at home. Often the two are combined for the best, longest-lasting result.

Comfort and safety

Because treatment is supervised by a dentist, the gums are protected and the gel strength is matched to your teeth — keeping sensitivity to a minimum. Shop-bought strips cannot offer that safeguard.

A short consultation will tell you what shade is realistically achievable for your teeth.
